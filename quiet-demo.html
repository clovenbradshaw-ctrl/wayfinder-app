3. Drive the same route again and again

Three junctions on one route. Each clean drive teaches it you know the road; one lucky pass is not enough. Exits keep a safety floor. A miss after a quiet prompt sends that junction back to full.
Familiarity is the lower end of a Wilson confidence interval over decayed passes (half-life 120 days). The safety floor is a declared rule outside the learner: exits, ramps, forks and roundabouts never drop below two prompts.
